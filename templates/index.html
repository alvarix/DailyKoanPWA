Settings

Notifications

A koan arrives as a push notification each morning. The bell in the corner does the same thing.

Koan mode

Hold keeps one koan for everyone until Next is pressed. Daily rotates to a new koan each day.

Koan order

In order walks the classic collections case by case. Random jumps somewhere new each Next.

Daily push time

Saved.

Your preferred delivery time (local). The server currently delivers around 08:00 UTC — moving the actual clock is an ops step on the droplet (deployment runbook).

Night mode

Saved on this device. System follows your appearance setting.

Enable Daily Koan Notifications

  1. Tap Share in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the app from your Home Screen.
  4. Tap the bell and allow notifications.
